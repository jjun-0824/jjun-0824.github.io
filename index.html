AI Engineer · Samsung Research, Samsung Electronics

I am an AI engineer on the post-training part of the AI Core Team at Samsung Research, where I work on agentic reinforcement learning for LLMs in simulated environments, synthetic data generation for agent training, turn-level credit assignment, and on-policy distillation for efficient reasoning.

I received my M.S. in Data Science from Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Taesup Kim, where I studied knowledge distillation for language models and self-supervised dense representation learning. Before that, I received B.S. degrees in Statistics and Computer Science from Sungkyunkwan University.

Research interests: LLM post-training, reinforcement learning for agents, knowledge distillation, efficient reasoning, on-device AI.
